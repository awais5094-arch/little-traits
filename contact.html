Helpful Answers

Frequently asked questions

How does delivery work in Pakistan?

Orders go out by tracked courier to Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, and all major cities. Flat-rate and free-delivery thresholds will be confirmed at launch.

What if the size doesn’t fit?

Exchange any unworn, unwashed piece with tags attached within 7 days. If your child is between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

Can I pay with Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery in PKR and direct bank transfer are both available at checkout.

How should I wash Little Traits pieces?

Everything is pre-washed to minimise shrinkage. Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with mild detergent and line dry in the shade.
