Before you ask

How does delivery work in Pakistan?

Delivery charges and available services will be shown before payment in the live store.

What if the size doesn’t fit?

Check the size chart before ordering and keep the garment’s tags attached when trying it on. The live store will display its exchange terms before checkout.

Can I pay with Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery in PKR and direct bank transfer are both available at checkout.

How should I wash Little Traits pieces?

Follow the care label on each garment. Wash similar colours together and check the recommended temperature before washing.
