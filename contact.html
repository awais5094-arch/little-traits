Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does nationwide delivery work in Pakistan?

Orders are dispatched via tracked courier across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, and all major cities. Exact flat-rate shipping and free-delivery thresholds will be finalized by the client at launch.

What if the size doesn’t fit my child?

Children grow quickly! We offer a simple 7-day size exchange on unworn, unwashed garments with original tags attached. If your child is between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

Can I pay with Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes. Both Cash on Delivery (COD) in PKR and Direct Bank Transfer are supported natively through WooCommerce checkout upon store launch.

How should I wash Little Traits garments?

All our combed cottons, muslins, and linen blends are pre-washed to minimize shrinkage. Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with mild detergent and line dry in the shade.
