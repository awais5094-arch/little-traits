Our Material Philosophy

Breathable weaves that feel gentle from the very first morning.

Children’s skin needs natural airflow—especially across Pakistan’s warm afternoons and breezy evenings. Instead of stiff synthetics or heavy embellishments, we focus on tactile fabrics and quiet details.

Pre-Washed Combed Cotton & Linen

Locally milled single-jersey, double-gauze muslin, and linen-cotton blends pre-washed so they stay true to size and soft against sensitive skin.

Natural Buttons & Tagless Necklines

Smooth coconut-shell and mother-of-pearl buttons paired with heat-pressed inner neck labels—never scratchy woven tags.

Room-to-Grow Tailoring

Generous hems, roll-tab cuffs, and soft elasticated waistbands let each garment adapt as your child grows through the season.
