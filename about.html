Meet Little Traits.

Clothes for the everyday part of growing up.

Little Traits is a children’s clothing store for families in Pakistan. You’ll find shirts, dresses, sets and baby layers, with boys’, girls’ and unisex collections to browse.

This first collection preview brings together six outfits for ages 6 months to 10 years. Choose by their measurements and the sizes available on each piece.

A little help choosing.

Start with their measurements. The age on a label is only a guide.

  • Check the fit

    Compare height, chest and waist with the size chart before choosing a size.

  • Build an outfit

    Browse sets for an outfit in one step, or choose a separate layer for cooler days.

  • Keep the care label

    Follow each garment’s washing instructions and wash similar colours together.
