Built for every little adventure, gentle from the first wear.

Why We Started

Everyday clothes that are easy to shop and lovely to wear.

Shopping for kids often means choosing between stiff, over-decorated occasion wear and basic tees that lose their shape after a few washes.

Little Traits sits in between. We design a small capsule of 10–15 seasonal pieces, which lets us give every garment extra care — from pre-washing our combed Pakistani cottons to choosing smooth coconut-shell buttons and pinch-free waistbands.

Inside Every Garment

The details you feel, wash after wash.

Every seam, pleat, and closure is chosen for active movement and sensitive skin.

  1. 01

    Breathable natural fabrics

    Combed cotton, double-gauze muslin, and linen blends, pre-washed so they stay true to size and cool through warm afternoons.

  2. 02

    Easy, fuss-free dressing

    Wide keyhole necklines, pull-on waists, and nickel-free baby snaps make mornings and nappy changes quicker.

  3. 03

    Finished to be handed down

    Enclosed French seams stop fraying and rough edges, so pieces stay soft enough to pass on to younger siblings.

  4. 04

    Gentle, azo-free dyes

    Water-based pigments keep colours rich without compromising the natural breathability of the cotton.
